### Batch 1 observations
- **Dialogue is the most common text (12 of 30 frames with text)**, always bottom-center over the 3D scene, with no box behind it. Bright scenes (frames 16, 21) give very low contrast. This confirms contrast boosting / white-text isolation as the top preprocessing need.
- **Typewriter effect**: frames 18 and 34 caught lines mid-reveal. The user will need to capture after the line finishes; worth a note in the README, and maybe a short delay option.
- **Inline key and mouse icons** appear in tutorials and hints (11, 27, 28). OCR may read them as letters (F, Q); they need filtering or editing.
- **Menus are the easiest text** (24, 32, 36): dark text on light panels, dense, lots of useful vocabulary (獲得, 累計, 属性, 謎解き, 装備).
- **Game-specific terms are frequent**: キボ, スターリンク, ステラストーン, アニマ, place and character names. These won't be in JMdict. Needed: a clear "not in dictionary" state, and later perhaps a user glossary.
- **Mixed numbers, counters and symbols** (7/20, 5.4%, （火）, x1) appear in quests and menus; the analyzer must handle them without breaking word splitting.
- **Playback speed menu and AUTO / skip buttons** sit top-right during dialogue; keep selections tight to avoid them.

## Conclusions for mc-jisho-lens

1. **Two main use cases:** story dialogue (bottom-center, no box) and menus / item descriptions (dark text on light parchment panels). Phase 0 OCR tests should cover both.
2. **Contrast is the main problem for dialogue.** About a quarter of dialogue frames had white text on bright backgrounds (cream, cyan, pale blue, white glow). Preprocessing to boost contrast and isolate white text is a must-have, not an option.
3. **Panels come in both light and dark themes.** Loading tips appear as dark-on-white and light-on-black versions of the same text, so preprocessing must handle both polarities (auto-detect and invert).
4. **Speaker name line:** always above the dialogue in a lighter blue; detect it and show it separately from the sentence.
5. **Typewriter text and fast-forward:** lines are often caught mid-reveal (×2.0 / ×3.0 speeds and AUTO are common), so capture after the line finishes; a short delay option would help.
6. **Inline icons:** key and mouse icons ([Q], [F], [T], mouse button) sit inside tutorial text and HUD prompts; filter single boxed letters or let the user edit them.
7. **Vocabulary is rich in compounds and game terms:** menus and descriptions are full of useful words (獲得, 累計, 属性, 消費, 修復, 生産効率, 共鳴), while names and coined terms (キボ, スターリンク, ステラカード, アニマ, 幽光の炎核) won't be in JMdict. A clear "not in dictionary" state, and later a user glossary, are worth planning.
8. **Combat HUD text is the hardest and least useful to capture** (tiny, moving, over effects); not worth optimizing for.
9. **Numbers and symbols** (7/20, 418%, +53, ×1, 【】, ——, ……) are frequent; the tokenizer and furigana renderer must pass them through cleanly.
